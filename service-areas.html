Service Areas

Based in Siletz, serving Lincoln County and the central Oregon Coast.

Tad Oliphant Construction serves homeowners throughout Lincoln County and the central Oregon Coast. Whether you're in a beachfront community facing salt-air exposure or an inland town dealing with persistent moisture, we bring the same quality craftsmanship and honest communication to every project.

Siletz - Home Base

Our home community in the Siletz River valley. We handle everything from deck builds and siding repairs to full remodels for homeowners in town and the surrounding rural areas.

View Services →

Newport

Lincoln County's largest city and a hub for coastal living. Oceanfront homes here face extreme wind and salt exposure - our most common jobs include siding replacement, dry-rot repair, and deck rebuilds.

View Services →

Lincoln City

Popular vacation-home destination stretching seven miles along the coast. We work with both full-time residents and out-of-town homeowners who need a trusted local contractor for repairs and remodels.

View Services →

Toledo

Situated along the Yaquina River just inland from Newport. Toledo homes benefit from slightly less salt exposure but still contend with heavy rainfall and moisture-related rot damage.

View Services →

Depoe Bay

Known for its dramatic coastline and whale watching. Properties here sit right at the ocean's edge, making weather-resistant construction and regular exterior maintenance essential.

View Services →

Waldport

A quiet community at the mouth of the Alsea River. Waldport homeowners commonly need deck construction, roofing, and window replacements built to handle the coastal climate.

View Services →

South Beach

The growing residential area south of the Yaquina Bay Bridge. Newer developments and older beach homes alike need contractors who understand the demands of oceanfront and near-ocean construction.

View Services →
