What We Do

Our Remodeling Services

Whether you've outgrown your floor plan, inherited a dated coastal property, or need to make your home safer and more functional as you age, we handle the full scope of residential remodeling in Lincoln County. Every project is managed directly by Tad - the same person who gives you the estimate is the one overseeing your build.

Whole-Home Renovation

Some homes need more than a single room update. We take on complete renovations - gutting interiors down to the studs, replacing outdated electrical and plumbing, installing new insulation, and rebuilding the space from the inside out. This is especially common with older coastal homes in Newport, Lincoln City, and Waldport where systems haven't been updated in decades. We coordinate every phase so you're not juggling multiple contractors or timelines.

Room Additions

When your family or lifestyle has outgrown your home's footprint, an addition is often more practical than moving - particularly on the Oregon Coast where available housing is limited. We build bedroom additions, expanded living areas, sunrooms, and bonus rooms. Every addition is engineered for the specific soil, wind, and moisture conditions at your property and permitted through Lincoln County.

Open Concept Conversions

Many coastal homes built in the 1960s through 1990s have chopped-up floor plans with small, separated rooms. We remove interior walls - including load-bearing walls with proper structural engineering - to create open-concept kitchens, living rooms, and dining areas. The result is a home that feels larger, gets better natural light, and functions the way modern families actually live.

Interior Updates

Not every remodel requires tearing the house apart. We handle targeted upgrades including new flooring, trim and baseboard replacement, drywall repair and finishing, interior painting, closet reconfiguration, and built-in shelving. These updates are a cost-effective way to modernize a tired interior without the timeline and investment of a full renovation.

Aging-in-Place Modifications

The Oregon Coast is home to a large and growing retirement community, and many homeowners want to stay in the house they love as they age. We install walk-in showers with grab bars, widen doorways for wheelchair access, add main-floor bedrooms and bathrooms, improve lighting, and eliminate tripping hazards. These modifications are designed to keep you safe and independent without making your home feel institutional.

Vacation Rental Upgrades

Lincoln County has one of the highest concentrations of vacation rentals on the Oregon Coast, and owners know that updated interiors drive higher nightly rates and better reviews. We renovate rental properties with durable, guest-friendly materials - luxury vinyl plank, quartz countertops, tile showers - that look great in listing photos and hold up to heavy use. We can also add features like outdoor living spaces and extra sleeping areas to increase capacity and booking appeal.

Coastal Construction Expertise

Remodeling Coastal Oregon Homes

Remodeling on the Oregon Coast is fundamentally different from inland work. The homes we open up regularly reveal problems that wouldn't exist 30 miles from the ocean - and addressing them correctly is the difference between a remodel that lasts and one that fails within a few years.

What We Find Behind the Walls

Many homes in Lincoln County were built with balloon framing, minimal moisture barriers, and insulation that has been absorbing coastal humidity for decades. When we open walls during demolition, we commonly find hidden moisture damage, outdated knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing that's corroded from the inside, and structural framing that's been quietly deteriorating behind intact-looking siding. This isn't unusual - it's the reality of coastal construction, and it's why we never quote a remodel without accounting for potential surprises.

How We Handle the Unexpected

When we find damage during demolition, we don't guess at solutions or pressure you into snap decisions. We stop, photograph the problem, explain what we're seeing, present your options with honest cost estimates, and wait for your approval before proceeding. After more than two decades of opening up coastal homes, very little surprises us - but we understand that it can surprise you, and we handle those conversations with transparency and patience.

Materials That Hold Up

Salt air accelerates corrosion on metal fasteners and hardware. Persistent moisture promotes mold and rot in organic materials. We specify stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized connectors, moisture-resistant drywall in bathrooms and kitchens, closed-cell spray foam where moisture intrusion is a risk, and finishes that resist the humidity swings coastal homes experience year-round. Every material choice is made with the understanding that this house sits in one of the most demanding environments in the Pacific Northwest.

Our Process

How We Manage Your Remodel

A remodel is one of the most disruptive things a homeowner goes through. We've refined our process over 20+ years and hundreds of projects to minimize that disruption while keeping quality high and communication clear.

Single Point of Contact

Tad is your project manager from the first phone call through final walkthrough. You're never handed off to a foreman you've never met or left wondering who's in charge. When you have a question, you call Tad directly. When a decision needs to be made on-site, Tad makes it - or calls you to discuss it if it affects your scope or budget.

Licensed Subcontractor Coordination

Remodels involve specialists - licensed electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, and sometimes structural engineers. We coordinate all subcontractors, schedule their work to avoid delays and conflicts, and ensure their work meets code before the next phase begins. You deal with one company, one schedule, and one invoice.

Permitting and Inspections

We handle the complete permitting process through Lincoln County - from initial plan submission through every required inspection to final sign-off. Permit requirements vary based on scope, and we know what triggers a permit and what doesn't. You won't need to visit the building department or figure out the code requirements yourself.

Phased Construction

For homeowners living in the house during a remodel, we plan the work in phases to keep disruption manageable. Demolition, rough-in, and finishing happen in a logical sequence. We protect finished areas from dust and debris, maintain access to at least one functional bathroom and kitchen whenever possible, and clean up at the end of every workday. A remodel doesn't have to mean living in a construction zone for months.

Clear Communication Throughout

You'll never wonder what's happening with your project. We provide regular progress updates, give advance notice before noisy or disruptive phases, and address concerns the same day they're raised. Our 54 five-star reviews consistently mention communication as one of the things homeowners appreciate most about working with us.
