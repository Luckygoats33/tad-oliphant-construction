How to Plan a Whole House Remodel on the Oregon Coast

8 min read

Home remodeling on the Oregon Coast comes with unique considerations that set it apart from renovation work anywhere else in the state. Moisture management, material selection, and coastal building code compliance all factor into every project we take on. Whether you are updating a kitchen, remodeling a bathroom, or renovating your entire home, understanding these factors will help you make better decisions.

Tad Oliphant Construction has been remodeling homes in Lincoln County since 2015. We handle projects of all sizes, from a single bathroom update to whole-home renovations. Every project gets the same attention to detail and the same commitment to doing the job right.

Kitchen Remodeling

Kitchens are consistently the highest-return renovation for homeowners. On the Oregon Coast, kitchen remodels also address common issues like poor ventilation, outdated electrical systems, and moisture-damaged cabinetry. We work with you to design a kitchen that looks great, functions well, and stands up to the coastal environment.

Our kitchen projects typically include demolition, structural modifications if needed, new cabinetry, countertops, flooring, lighting, plumbing, and electrical work. We coordinate all trades and manage the entire project timeline from start to finish.

Bathroom Remodeling

Bathrooms in coastal homes are particularly vulnerable to moisture damage. The combination of high indoor humidity from showers and the ambient moisture from our marine environment creates conditions that accelerate mold growth and deteriorate standard materials. Every bathroom remodel we do includes proper moisture barriers, exhaust ventilation, and moisture-resistant materials.

We recommend cement board substrate behind all shower and tub surrounds, waterproof membrane systems, and exhaust fans ducted directly to the exterior. These details prevent the hidden moisture damage that we regularly find in older coastal bathrooms.

Permits and Building Codes

Most remodeling projects in Lincoln County require permits, especially any work that involves structural changes, electrical, plumbing, or changes to the building envelope. We handle all permit applications and inspections, ensuring your project meets current Oregon building code requirements.

Coastal construction in our area may also need to comply with flood zone regulations, shoreline setback requirements, and wind load specifications. We are familiar with all of these requirements and factor them into our project planning.

Timeline and Process

A typical bathroom remodel takes two to three weeks. Kitchen remodels generally run four to eight weeks depending on scope. Whole-home renovations vary widely but typically take two to six months. We provide a detailed timeline before work begins and keep you updated on progress throughout the project.
