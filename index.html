Coastal Construction Specialists

Oregon Coast Homes Demand More

Living on the Oregon Coast means your home faces conditions most inland contractors never deal with - sustained Pacific winds, salt-laden air, 60+ inches of annual rainfall, and the persistent moisture that leads to hidden rot in walls, decks, and structural framing.

After two decades of working exclusively in Lincoln County, we've learned exactly what fails first, which materials hold up, and how to build structures that last in this environment. When we pull off old siding and find rot behind it, that's not a surprise - it's the reason you called us.
