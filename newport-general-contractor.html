General Contractor Serving Newport, Oregon

Newport is the largest city in Lincoln County and the commercial hub of the central Oregon Coast. From the working waterfront of the Bayfront district to the residential neighborhoods along the bluffs, Newport's homes face some of the most demanding weather conditions on the Pacific seaboard. We're based just 15 miles inland in Siletz and have been building and repairing homes throughout Newport since 2015.

The city straddles Yaquina Bay, with the iconic Yaquina Bay Bridge connecting the north and south sides of town. Newport's housing stock ranges from historic Nye Beach cottages built in the early 1900s to modern oceanfront homes perched on coastal bluffs. The Bayfront, Agate Beach, and Nye Beach neighborhoods each present distinct construction challenges - and we know them all.

Newport also has a significant vacation rental market, with property owners who need reliable contractors to maintain and upgrade rental units between seasons. We've become a go-to contractor for property managers and out-of-town owners who need someone they can trust to manage projects without constant supervision.

Construction Challenges Specific to Newport

Newport's direct ocean exposure creates construction demands that inland contractors aren't equipped to handle:

  • Salt air corrosion - Oceanfront and near-ocean properties experience relentless salt spray that corrodes metal fasteners, eats through cheap paint, and accelerates wood deterioration. We use marine-grade hardware, stainless steel fasteners, and coatings rated for salt exposure.
  • Wind-driven rain damage - Pacific storms push rain horizontally into siding, window seams, and roof transitions. Standard weatherproofing fails here. We install flashing systems and siding details designed for wind-driven moisture infiltration.
  • Vacation rental wear and tear - High-turnover rental properties in Newport take a beating. Decks, interior finishes, and bathroom fixtures need to be durable enough for heavy use while still looking good for guests. We build with durability-first materials.
  • Bluff and slope construction - Many Newport homes sit on elevated lots with ocean views. Deck construction and foundation work on slopes requires proper engineering, soil assessment, and drainage planning that flat-lot builders don't think about.
  • Historic home renovation - Nye Beach and older Newport neighborhoods have homes with original wood siding, single-pane windows, and dated electrical. We renovate these properties to modern standards while respecting their character.
