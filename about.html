About Tad Oliphant Construction

Owner-operated general contracting built on trust, transparency, and two decades of coastal construction expertise.

Our Story

Owner-Operated Since 2015

Tad Oliphant Construction LLC was founded with a straightforward idea: do honest work, communicate clearly, and stand behind every project. Tad started the company right here in Lincoln County, and that's where he's stayed - building and repairing homes for the people who live on the Oregon Coast.

Unlike larger outfits that send a crew and disappear, Tad is hands-on at every job. He's the one who writes your estimate, shows up on day one, and stays through the final walkthrough. That personal accountability is why 54 customers have left five-star Google reviews.

Why Coastal Building Is Different

Oregon Coast Construction Expertise

Lincoln County averages over 60 inches of rain annually. Salt-laden air corrodes fasteners and degrades finishes. Sustained Pacific winds stress roofing, siding, and fencing in ways inland homes never experience.

After more than two decades working exclusively in this environment, we know which composite decking holds up to salt air, how moisture trapped behind siding leads to structural rot, and which roofing materials resist 70+ mph coastal gusts. This isn't textbook knowledge - it's experience built through hundreds of projects.

How We Work

Our Approach to Every Project

  • 01

    Honest Communication

    We don't upsell or sugarcoat. If we find hidden damage, we show you exactly what's going on, explain your options, and give you a clear cost breakdown before proceeding.

  • 02

    Transparent Pricing

    Our estimates break down materials, labor, and timeline. If something changes during the project, we discuss it with you before the cost changes. No surprises on the invoice.

  • 03

    We Stand Behind Our Work

    When we finish a project, we don't disappear. If something isn't right, we come back and make it right. That accountability is why homeowners hire us again and refer us to their neighbors.

  • 04

    On Time, On Budget

    We set realistic timelines and stick to them. Oregon Coast weather can be unpredictable, but our scheduling accounts for that. Customers consistently report we finish when expected.
