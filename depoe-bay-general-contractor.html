General Contractor Serving Depoe Bay, Oregon

Depoe Bay is famous for claiming the "world's smallest navigable harbor" - a narrow channel carved into the basalt cliffs where the Pacific forces its way into a tiny protected basin. The town perches on dramatic oceanfront cliffs between Lincoln City and Newport, and its homes occupy some of the most spectacular (and most exposed) real estate on the Oregon Coast.

Properties in Depoe Bay aren't just near the ocean - many are directly above it. Homes sit on basalt bluffs with waves crashing below, salt spray coating windows and siding daily, and wind that never truly stops. The famous "spouting horns" that draw tourists also demonstrate the raw power of the sea that Depoe Bay homes must withstand year after year.

This is a community of premium vacation properties and permanent residents who chose Depoe Bay for its dramatic beauty and accept the maintenance demands that come with it. We've been working on Depoe Bay properties since 2015, and we understand that construction here isn't just about building - it's about engineering homes to survive an environment that actively tries to tear them apart.

Construction Challenges Specific to Depoe Bay

Depoe Bay presents the most extreme construction conditions in our service area:

  • Extreme salt spray exposure - Cliff-top homes in Depoe Bay receive constant salt spray - not just salt air, but actual ocean water carried by wind. Standard metal hardware corrodes within months. We use exclusively stainless steel and marine-grade fasteners, and we spec coatings that are designed for direct maritime exposure.
  • Cliff-side structural challenges - Building on basalt bluffs requires specialized knowledge of anchoring, drainage, and soil stability. Decks and exterior structures must be engineered for both the wind loads and the unstable edges of coastal bluffs. We work with structural engineers when the site demands it.
  • Sustained wind damage - Depoe Bay sits on an exposed headland that channels Pacific wind through the town. Roofing, siding, and fencing must be installed to withstand gusts that regularly exceed 60 mph during winter storms. Standard installation methods fail here.
  • Premium property expectations - Depoe Bay vacation homes and permanent residences represent significant investments. Owners expect - and deserve - construction quality that matches the property's value. We use premium materials and finish details appropriate for high-end coastal homes.
  • Access and logistics - Many Depoe Bay properties have limited access due to narrow cliff-top lots, steep driveways, and tight neighborhood streets. Material delivery and equipment staging require advance planning that we factor into every Depoe Bay project.
