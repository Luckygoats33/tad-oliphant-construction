General Contractor Serving Toledo, Oregon

Toledo sits along the Yaquina River about six miles inland from Newport, serving as the county seat of Lincoln County. It's a working-class community with roots in the timber and paper industries, and its housing stock reflects that practical heritage - solid but often dated homes built for function rather than flash. Toledo offers some of the most affordable housing in Lincoln County, which means many homeowners here are looking for cost-effective renovation rather than luxury upgrades.

The town has seen a quiet resurgence in recent years, attracting artists, small business owners, and families priced out of coastal communities. The main street has galleries and shops, and the neighborhoods surrounding downtown feature a mix of mid-century homes, bungalows, and newer construction. Toledo's proximity to Newport - just a 10-minute drive down Highway 20 - gives residents coastal access without coastal housing prices.

We're based in Siletz, just a short drive north, and Toledo is one of our most frequent service areas. We understand that Toledo homeowners want quality work at a fair price, and that many projects here involve bringing older homes up to modern comfort and efficiency standards rather than building from scratch.

Construction Challenges Specific to Toledo

Toledo's inland river location creates a different set of construction challenges than the beachfront towns:

  • Yaquina River moisture - Properties along the Yaquina River and its tributaries sit in a persistent moisture zone. Crawl spaces stay damp, foundation perimeters hold water, and subfloor framing is prone to rot. We address moisture at the source, not just the symptoms.
  • Aging home stock - Many Toledo homes date to the 1940s through 1970s. They have original single-pane windows, outdated electrical panels, deteriorating siding, and structural framing that's been quietly rotting for decades. We specialize in bringing these homes back to life.
  • Budget-conscious renovations - Toledo homeowners typically need practical, value-driven improvements. We provide honest assessments of what's truly necessary versus what's cosmetic, and we prioritize structural integrity and efficiency over premium finishes when budgets are tight.
  • Heavy rainfall and drainage - Toledo receives over 70 inches of rain annually. Poor drainage around foundations is one of the most common issues we see - water pooling in crawl spaces, saturated soil pushing against foundations, and gutters that can't handle the volume.
  • Conversion and addition projects - With Toledo's affordable property prices, many homeowners choose to renovate and expand rather than move. We handle room additions, garage conversions, and layout reconfigurations that make existing homes work better for their families.
