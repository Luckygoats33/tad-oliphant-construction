Seasonal Home Maintenance Checklist for Oregon Coast Owners

6 min read

Preventive maintenance is the single most cost-effective thing you can do to protect your coastal Oregon home. A small investment in regular inspections and upkeep can prevent thousands of dollars in repair costs and extend the life of every component of your home. After more than two decades of construction work in Lincoln County, we have seen the difference that consistent maintenance makes.

Tad Oliphant Construction provides maintenance guidance and services for homeowners throughout Lincoln County. Whether you need a professional inspection, seasonal maintenance work, or advice on what to prioritize, we are here to help.

Spring Maintenance Checklist

After the winter storm season, spring is the time to assess any damage and prepare your home for the drier months. Key tasks include inspecting your roof for missing or damaged shingles, checking all gutters and downspouts for debris and proper flow, examining siding for new cracks, gaps, or peeling paint, testing all windows and doors for proper operation and seal, inspecting decks for soft spots, loose fasteners, or structural movement, and checking the crawl space for standing water or new moisture issues.

Summer Projects

Summer is the ideal time for exterior maintenance and improvements. The drier weather allows paint and stain to cure properly, and sealants can achieve their full bond before the rain returns. If you have any exterior work planned, schedule it during the May through September window for the best results.

This is also the best time for deck staining, exterior painting, caulking and sealing around windows and doors, roof moss treatment, and any repair work that requires dry conditions.

Fall Preparation

Before the fall rains begin in earnest, take time to prepare your home for the wet season. Clean all gutters thoroughly, check that downspout extensions are directing water away from the foundation, trim any tree branches that overhang the roof or are close to the siding, and make sure all exterior caulking and sealant is in good condition.

Winter Storm Readiness

The most important winter maintenance task is keeping an eye on your home during and after storms. Check for new leaks, look for missing shingles or siding after high winds, and address any damage immediately. Small problems caught early are simple repairs. Small problems left for months become big problems.

Vacation Home Maintenance

If your Oregon Coast property is a vacation home or rental, regular maintenance is even more critical. Unoccupied homes can develop problems that go undetected for weeks or months. A small roof leak that would be noticed immediately in an occupied home can cause extensive damage in a vacant property before anyone discovers it.

We recommend having a local professional inspect your property at least twice a year, in spring and fall. We provide this service for vacation homeowners throughout Lincoln County.
